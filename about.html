Hi, I'm Ben!

B.A. Design & UX Design, Michigan blevitsky.github.io/blevitsky

I make things. Apps, websites, prototypes, and at one point a 3D-printed penguin. I write my own code instead of stopping at Figma, mostly because clicking through a mockup and actually building the interaction teach you two different lessons.

Real design work so far: a design system for a national contractor, enterprise creative at an agency, a diabetes-tracking app prototype. Same loop every time — talk to people, figure out what they actually meant, build something, watch someone use it, realize half my assumptions were wrong, adjust. Outside of work I follow politics more than most people my age and read a lot of history. Same muscle as research, honestly, just pointed at the past instead of a user interview.

3 words for how I work: build it real.

My time at Michigan

B.A. in Design and UX Design.

Michigan's design program stays broad for a long time before it lets you specialize. I leaned into that instead of fighting it. My minors ended up being Product Design, Software Engineering, Graphic Design, and Industrial Design — a strange combination on paper, but it's exactly the mix I use in practice. The software side is why I ship real code instead of static comps. The industrial design side is why MistMates exists as an actual CAD-modeled object, not just a rendering.

Four classes actually changed how I work: Bits and Atoms, Product Design, Typography, Intro to UX. The first two pushed me to prototype physically as often as digitally. The last two are the unglamorous fundamentals — they show up in every case study on this site whether anyone notices or not.

Before any of that there was a Stamps Summer Studio: Drawing, 2D, 3D, 4D, ending in a portfolio review from working artists. It's the reason "designer who also draws" is actually true of me, not just a line on a resume.
