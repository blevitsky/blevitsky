Soothe app - ready for Vercel

To put it live:
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open Terminal and go into the folder: type "cd " (with a space), drag the
   soothe-app folder into the Terminal window, and press Enter.
3. Run:  npx vercel
   Log in when asked and accept the defaults. You get a preview link.
4. Run:  npx vercel --prod
   This gives you the final public link (something like soothe-app.vercel.app).

Everything people save in the app stays in their own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
